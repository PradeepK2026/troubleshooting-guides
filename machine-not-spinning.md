# Knowledge Base Article: Washing Machine Not Spinning

**Document ID:** WM-TRO-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-08  
**Audience:** End Users, Support Technicians  
**Category:** Troubleshooting Guide  

## 1. Purpose
Provide structured troubleshooting for washing machines that fail to spin during the spin cycle.

## 2. Scope
Applicable to top-load and front-load washing machines. Non-invasive troubleshooting intended for end users; internal servicing must be performed by authorized personnel.

## 3. Symptoms
- Drum does not rotate  
- Water remains in the drum  
- Clothes remain wet  

## 4. Potential Causes
- Overloaded or unbalanced laundry  
- Clogged drain filter or hose  
- Lid/door not fully closed  
- Incorrect cycle selection  
- Faulty internal components  

## 5. Troubleshooting Procedure
1. **Verify Load Distribution** – Redistribute laundry; avoid overloading.  
2. **Inspect Drainage System** – Clean filter; check hose.  
3. **Confirm Lid/Door Lock** – Ensure full closure.  
4. **Power Reset** – Turn off, unplug 5 minutes, restart.  
5. **Escalation** – Contact authorized service personnel if unresolved.

## 6. Preventive Measures
- Avoid overloading  
- Clean drain filter regularly  
- Use recommended cycles  
- Place on a level surface
